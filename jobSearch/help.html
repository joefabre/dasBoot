Getting Started

Welcome to JobTracker Pro!

JobTracker Pro is your comprehensive job search companion, designed to help you organize applications, manage professional contacts, and track your career journey progress.

First Steps

  1. Explore the Interface: Familiarize yourself with the four main sections: Dashboard, Applications, Contacts, and Analytics
  2. Add Your First Application: Start by adding a job application to see how the system works
  3. Set Up Contacts: Add contact information for recruiters and hiring managers
  4. Review Analytics: Check your progress and insights as you add more data

Dashboard

Your command center showing key statistics, recent activity, and upcoming tasks at a glance.

Applications

Track all your job applications with detailed information and status updates.

Contacts

Manage your professional network and link contacts to specific applications.

Analytics

Gain insights into your job search patterns and track your success rates.

Managing Applications

Adding New Applications

  1. Click the "Add Application" button
  2. Fill in the required fields:
    • Job Title (required)
    • Company (required)
  3. Add optional information:
    • Location
    • Salary range
    • Job URL
    • Notes
  4. Include contact information if available
  5. Set the current status
  6. Click "Save Application"

Application Statuses

  • Interested: Jobs you're considering applying to
  • Applied: Applications you've submitted
  • Interview: Interviews scheduled or completed (enables interview scheduling fields)
  • Offer: Job offers received
  • Rejected: Applications that were declined
  • Accepted: Offers you've accepted

Interview Scheduling

When you set an application status to "Interview", additional fields will appear to help you manage your interview details:

Interview Fields:

  • Interview Date: The date of your interview
  • Interview Time: The time of your interview
  • Interview Type: Phone, Video, In-Person, Panel, or Technical
  • Duration: How long the interview is expected to last (in minutes)
  • Location/Meeting Link: Office address or video call URL
  • Interview Notes: Preparation notes, questions to ask, research about the company

Smart Clock Alerts:

The footer clock provides visual alerts for your scheduled interviews:

  • Red Flashing: Appears 1 hour before any scheduled interview
  • Green Flashing: Appears during the interview time period
  • Normal Display: Returns to normal after the interview ends

Contact Integration

When adding applications, you can include contact information for hiring managers or recruiters. This information will automatically create or update contacts in your network, making it easy to track who you've been in touch with for each position.

Contact Fields:

  • Contact Name
  • Contact Title (e.g., "Hiring Manager")
  • Contact Email
  • Contact Phone
  • Contact LinkedIn

Filtering Applications

Use the filter options to find specific applications:

  • Status Filter: Show only applications with specific statuses
  • Date Filter: Display applications added after a certain date

Interview Scheduling System

Complete Interview Management

JobTracker Pro includes a comprehensive interview scheduling system that helps you manage all aspects of your interviews, from initial scheduling to timing alerts.

Setting Up Interviews

  1. Open an application or create a new one
  2. Set the status to "Interview"
  3. Interview scheduling fields will automatically appear
  4. Fill in the interview details (see below)
  5. Save the application

Interview Details

Required Information:

  • Interview Date: Select the date using the date picker
  • Interview Time: Enter the exact time of your interview

Optional Information:

  • Interview Type: Choose from:
    • Phone Interview
    • Video Interview
    • In-Person Interview
    • Panel Interview
    • Technical Interview
  • Duration: Expected length in minutes (default: 60 minutes)
  • Location/Meeting Link: Office address or video call URL
  • Interview Notes: Preparation notes, questions to ask, company research

Smart Clock System

The footer clock provides real-time alerts for your scheduled interviews:

Pre-Interview Alert

Clock flashes red starting 1 hour before any scheduled interview to give you preparation time.

Active Interview

Clock flashes green during the entire duration of your interview time.

Enhanced Interview Cards

Applications with scheduled interviews display with special styling and additional information:

Interview Card Features:

  • Orange Accent: Special border and background gradient
  • Interview Details Box: Highlighted section with all interview information
  • Smart Date Display: Shows "Today", "Tomorrow", or formatted date
  • Quick Actions: Context-aware buttons based on interview type:
    • "Join Call" for video interviews
    • "Call" for phone interviews
    • "Location" for in-person interviews
  • Contact Information: Interviewer details prominently displayed
  • Interview Notes: Preparation notes in a separate section

Best Practices

Before the Interview:

  • Fill in all interview details as soon as you schedule
  • Add detailed preparation notes
  • Include interviewer contact information
  • Test video call links in advance
  • Set a buffer time for technical issues

Using the Clock Alerts:

  • Red flash means you have 1 hour to prepare
  • Use this time for final preparation and setup
  • Green flash confirms you're in the interview window
  • Clock returns to normal when interview time ends

Interview Types Guide

Phone Interview:

  • Include phone number in location field
  • Note if you're calling them or they're calling you
  • Prepare in a quiet environment

Video Interview:

  • Add meeting link to location field
  • Test your camera and microphone beforehand
  • Check lighting and background
  • Have backup communication method ready

In-Person Interview:

  • Include full address in location field
  • Plan travel time and parking
  • Dress code considerations
  • Bring printed copies of resume

Contact Management

Building Your Professional Network

Contacts are automatically created when you add contact information to job applications, but you can also manually add contacts to build your professional network.

Adding Contacts

Automatic Contact Creation:

  • When adding job applications, include contact information
  • Contacts are automatically created or updated
  • Duplicate contacts are prevented by email matching

Manual Contact Addition:

  1. Go to the Contacts section
  2. Click "Add Contact"
  3. Fill in the contact information
  4. Add notes about how you know them or interaction history
  5. Save the contact

Contact Features

  • Alphabetical Sorting: Contacts are automatically sorted by name
  • Proper Capitalization: Names and titles are automatically formatted
  • Quick Communication: Click email or phone to contact directly
  • LinkedIn Integration: Quick access to LinkedIn profiles

Professional Print Reports

Generate Professional Reports

JobTracker Pro includes a comprehensive print system that allows you to create professional reports of your job search data. Perfect for sharing with career counselors, maintaining offline records, or creating portfolio documentation.

Accessing the Print Feature

  1. Click the Print button in the header navigation
  2. The print modal will open with various options
  3. Select categories and format preferences
  4. Click "Generate Print View"

Print Categories

Choose which information to include in your report:

Job Applications

Complete listing of all your job applications with status, dates, contact info, and interview details.

Professional Contacts

Your complete professional network with contact details, companies, and relationship notes.

Analytics Summary

Key statistics, status breakdowns, and personalized insights about your job search progress.

Print Format Options

Spreadsheet Format (Tables)

  • Applications Table: Job Title, Company, Status, Location, Salary, Date Added, Contact, Interview
  • Contacts Table: Name, Title, Company, Email, Phone, LinkedIn
  • Compact Layout: Maximum information in minimal space
  • Easy Scanning: Perfect for quick reference and sharing

Card Format (Detailed)

  • Complete Information: All details including notes, URLs, and interview specifics
  • Application Cards: Full job details with interview information and contact data
  • Contact Cards: Complete contact profiles with notes and relationship details
  • Readable Layout: Easy-to-read format for detailed review

Report Features

Professional Headers

  • Report title: "JobTracker Pro - Career Report"
  • Generation date and time
  • Summary statistics (total applications and contacts)
  • Clean, professional appearance

Smart Organization

  • Applications: Sorted by date (newest first)
  • Contacts: Sorted alphabetically by name
  • Status Colors: Application statuses color-coded for easy identification
  • Page Breaks: Automatic page breaks between sections

Interview Integration

  • Interview dates and times clearly displayed
  • Interview types (Phone, Video, In-Person, etc.)
  • Contact information linked to interviews
  • Interview preparation notes included

Print Best Practices

Before Printing:

  • Ensure all data is up-to-date
  • Review application statuses for accuracy
  • Update contact information
  • Add any missing notes or details

Choosing the Right Format:

  • Spreadsheet: Best for quick overviews and sharing
  • Card Format: Best for detailed reviews and portfolio inclusion
  • Mixed Approach: Generate both formats for different purposes

Using Print Reports:

  • Career Counseling: Share with career advisors for guidance
  • Interview Preparation: Quick reference for company research
  • Networking Events: Contact list for follow-up opportunities
  • Progress Tracking: Monthly or quarterly progress reports
  • Job Search Documentation: Maintain records for future reference

Technical Notes

Print Process:

  1. Report generates in a new browser window
  2. Print dialog opens automatically
  3. Window closes after printing (or manual close)
  4. Original application remains open and functional

Browser Compatibility:

  • Works with all modern browsers
  • Print styles optimized for standard paper sizes
  • Status colors preserved in compatible browsers
  • Automatic page breaks for clean printing

Troubleshooting Print Issues:

  • If print dialog doesn't appear, manually trigger print (Ctrl+P)
  • For color printing, ensure browser print settings allow colors
  • If content is cut off, check browser print scale settings
  • For best results, use "More settings" → "Print backgrounds"

Data Management

Data Storage

JobTracker Pro stores all your data locally in your browser using HTML5 LocalStorage. This means:

  • Your data stays private and secure on your device
  • No internet connection required after initial load
  • Data persists between browser sessions
  • No external servers or databases involved

Data Backup & Export

To Export Your Data:

  1. Click the Settings button (gear icon)
  2. Click "Export Data"
  3. A JSON file will be downloaded with all your data
  4. Store this file safely as a backup

Data Import & Restore

To Import Data:

  1. Click the Settings button
  2. Click "Choose File" under Import Data
  3. Select your exported JSON file
  4. Your data will be restored

Data Sync

The Sync button currently simulates cloud synchronization. In future versions, this may connect to cloud storage services for automatic backup.

Clearing Data

Warning: The "Clear All Data" option permanently removes all applications and contacts. Only use this if you want to start completely fresh.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

Data Not Saving

  • Check if your browser allows LocalStorage
  • Ensure you're not in private/incognito mode
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Check browser storage settings

Analytics Not Updating

  • Add more applications (minimum 1 required)
  • Switch to another section and back to Analytics
  • Refresh the page

Contact Information Not Displaying

  • Ensure contact name is entered in the application form
  • Check that the contact was saved properly
  • Verify the contact appears in the Contacts section

Application Filters Not Working

  • Clear all filters and try again
  • Check that applications exist with the selected criteria
  • Refresh the page to reset filters

Interview Fields Not Appearing

  • Ensure application status is set to "Interview"
  • Save and reopen the application if fields don't appear
  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Check that JavaScript is enabled in your browser

Clock Not Flashing for Interviews

  • Verify interview date and time are correctly entered
  • Check that your system clock is accurate
  • Ensure interview status is "Interview" not another status
  • Wait a few seconds for the clock to update
  • Refresh the page if clock appears stuck

Interview Details Not Displaying on Cards

  • Confirm interview date and time are filled in
  • Check that application status is "Interview"
  • Save the application and refresh the applications page
  • Verify interview information was properly saved

Print Feature Not Working

  • Ensure you have data to print (applications or contacts)
  • Check that at least one category is selected in print modal
  • Verify your browser allows pop-ups from this site
  • Try manually triggering print with Ctrl+P if dialog doesn't appear
  • Disable pop-up blockers for the print feature

Print Content Issues

  • If colors don't print, enable "Print backgrounds" in browser settings
  • For cut-off content, adjust print scale in browser print settings
  • If tables are too wide, try "Card Format" instead of "Spreadsheet Format"
  • Check paper size settings match your printer
  • Use landscape orientation for wide content

Browser Compatibility

JobTracker Pro works best on modern browsers:

  • Chrome 70+
  • Firefox 65+
  • Safari 12+
  • Edge 79+

Performance Tips

  • Regular data exports for backup
  • Clear browser cache if experiencing issues
  • Use consistent data entry for better analytics
  • Keep contact information up to date
